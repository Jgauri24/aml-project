## Keys and joins

Sales day **column names** become `d` on reshape; join `d` to calendar (many sales rows to one calendar day). Then join `(item_id, store_id, wm_yr_wk)` to prices (many daily rows to one weekly price). Both joins enforce many-to-one cardinality. No unmatched calendar days are accepted.

## Canonical panel

`series_id = item_id + '__' + store_id` has no validation/evaluation suffix. `units_sold` is observed unit sales, not latent unconstrained demand. Each selected series has every indexed daily row. Observed zeros stay zero; missing targets stay missing. `date` is a naive daily timestamp; `day_num` is the integer in `d_N`. Static identifiers and event strings use categorical dtypes; day/year integers, float32 targets/prices and boolean flags keep memory bounded.

`is_zero_sale` is nullable when the target is missing. `price_missing` means no known numeric listing for that row. `possible_prelaunch` is a past-only uncertain proxy: no positive sale and no price yet observed through that day. `availability_unknown` is always true: M5 has no stock inventory field. No confirmed stockout or launch label is inferred. Historical `snap` picks the appropriate state flag; original `snap_CA/TX/WI` are retained.

## Selection

Only d_1–d_1801 are inspected for selection and EDA. Rank by trailing 365-day mean, break ties by series_id, split into three rank terciles, then seeded round-robin sampling across store/category strata (80 per group, seed 42). Complete observed history is required by default; no positive-sales filter is applied, preserving sparse/all-zero candidates. The sample is deliberately stratified and is not population-representative. Exact eligibility, pool boundaries and compromises are in `data/interim/selection_metadata.json`.
